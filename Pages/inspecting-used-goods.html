Inspecting Used Goods

A quick checklist for inspecting a used item before you buy it locally.

Before you meet the seller

  • Ask for extra photos, including any damage or wear.
  • Confirm the item's age, condition and reason for selling.
  • Agree on a public meeting place for the handover.

What to check in person

Electronics

Power it on, check the screen, ports and battery health before paying.

Vehicles

Check the registration documents match the seller, and take a short test drive.

Furniture

Inspect joints, upholstery and any signs of pests or water damage.
