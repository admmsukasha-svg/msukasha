Inspecting Used Goods

Home/Inspection Guide

Before handing over any money for a used item, a proper inspection is your strongest protection. This guide covers the key checks for the most common categories on MSukasha's C2C marketplace.

Smartphones & Tablets

Dial *#06# — verify IMEI matches the box and receipt.
Check battery health — iOS: Settings → Battery. Android: Use AccuBattery app. Reject below 75%.
Test all ports — charge port, headphone jack, SIM tray, volume and power buttons.
Screen test — open a white background to spot dead pixels, yellow patches, or burn-in.
Red flag: Any device that cannot be factory reset in front of you may be iCloud/Google-locked.

Laptops & Computers

Boot the laptop and run it for 10 minutes. Check fan noise, heat, and startup time.
Press every key on the keyboard. Open Notepad to test typing. Check touchpad click and gestures.
Check the screen at full brightness for dead pixels, backlight bleed, or color inconsistency.
Run a battery test — Windows: powercfg /batteryreport in Command Prompt.
Test all USB ports, HDMI, headphone jack, and webcam.
Red flag: Unusual smells, overheating within minutes, or a seller refusing to log into Windows.

Vehicles (Cars & Motorcycles)

Inspect in daylight — check for mismatched paint, uneven panel gaps (sign of accident repair).
Open the hood — check for leaks, rust, or signs of flood damage inside engine bay.
Take a test drive for at least 10 minutes. Listen for knocking, vibrations, and test all gears.
Verify chassis number against all documents (registration, insurance). Numbers must match exactly.
Red flag: Seller refuses a test drive, documents don't match, or ownership transfer is "in process".

Furniture

Check all joints and connections — wiggle chairs, tables, and sofa frames for loose parts.
Inspect wooden surfaces for termite damage — small holes or fine sawdust are warning signs.
Smell the item — musty odors suggest dampness or mold inside cushioning or wood.
Check drawer slides, door hinges, and any mechanical parts (recliner mechanisms, foldable beds).

Always Meet Safely

Conduct all inspections in a public place with good lighting. Read our full C2C Safety Tips before meeting any seller.
