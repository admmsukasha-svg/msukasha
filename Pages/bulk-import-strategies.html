Bulk Import Strategies

Practical strategies for sourcing and importing products at wholesale volume.

Plan before you order

Bulk sourcing rewards preparation. A little planning up front saves money and prevents costly mistakes on large orders.

Define your MOQ

Know the minimum order quantity you can realistically sell or use.

Compare suppliers

Request quotes from 2–3 suppliers for the same product spec.

Sample first

Order a small sample before committing to a full bulk order.

Confirm logistics

Agree delivery timeline and cost before final payment.

Reducing landed cost

  • Consolidate orders across product lines with the same supplier to meet better price tiers.
  • Ask suppliers about seasonal pricing — off-season orders are often cheaper.
  • Factor delivery and any applicable taxes into your total cost, not just the unit price.
