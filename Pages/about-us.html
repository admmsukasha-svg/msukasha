About msukasha

Pakistan's platform for B2B wholesale sourcing and C2C local trading, built to make buying and selling simpler nationwide.

Who we are

msukasha is operated by MSUKASHA SMC-PRIVATE LIMITED, an SECP-registered company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. We connect verified wholesale suppliers with business buyers, and everyday people with local buyers for used goods — on a single platform.

What we're building

B2B Marketplace

Wholesale sourcing from verified suppliers, with trade assurance on eligible orders.

C2C Classifieds

Free local listings so anyone can buy and sell used items nearby.

Business Advertising

A way for businesses to promote products and offers directly on msukasha.
