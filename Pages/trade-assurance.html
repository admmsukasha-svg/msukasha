What Trade Assurance covers

Trade Assurance is msukasha's framework for giving buyers confidence when ordering from wholesale suppliers, and giving suppliers a clear, fair process for getting paid.

Order Protection

Agreed price, quantity and delivery terms are recorded before payment is released to the supplier.

On-Time Delivery

Suppliers commit to a delivery window agreed with the buyer at order confirmation.

Product Quality

Buyers can raise a quality dispute if goods received do not match what was agreed.

Dispute Resolution

The msukasha team mediates disputes between buyer and supplier before funds are finally released.

How it works

Place your order

Agree on price, MOQ and delivery terms with a verified supplier.

Payment is held

Your payment is held securely until the order terms are fulfilled.

Goods are delivered

You inspect the goods against the agreed order details.

Funds are released

Once you confirm, payment is released to the supplier.

Note: Trade Assurance is being rolled out as msukasha's payment backend goes live. Suppliers marked "Trade Assurance" on their listing currently follow these terms on a supported-order basis — confirm coverage with the supplier before paying.
