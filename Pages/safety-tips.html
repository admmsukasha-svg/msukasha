Home/Safety Tips
Always exercise caution when buying or selling locally. These tips will keep you safe.

Meet in a Public Place

Always meet buyers or sellers in a busy, well-lit public area — such as a café, mall, or market. Avoid private residences, especially for your first meeting.

Inspect Before You Pay

Test the item thoroughly before handing over any money. Check that electronics power on, vehicles start, and items match the photos in the listing.

Never Pay in Advance to Strangers

Do not send advance payment before seeing the item unless using our verified Trade Assurance escrow service. Scammers often ask for deposits before disappearing.

Bring Someone With You

For high-value items like vehicles or large electronics, bring a friend or family member. Never meet alone with strangers for expensive transactions.

Report Suspicious Activity

If a deal seems too good to be true, it probably is. Report suspicious listings using the Report button, or contact our support team immediately.
