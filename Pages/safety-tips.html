Safety Tips for Local Deals

Simple precautions to keep your local buying and selling experience safe.

Meet in public

Choose a busy, well-lit public place for the handover.

Bring someone

Where possible, bring a friend or family member along.

Pay in person

Avoid sending payment before you have seen and checked the item.

Keep chat on msukasha

Keep early conversation on the platform so there is a record of the listing.

If something goes wrong

If a listing or user seems suspicious, avoid the meetup and contact msukasha support.
