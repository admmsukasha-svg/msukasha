Bargaining is a normal and expected part of C2C transactions in Pakistan. But done poorly, it can lead to bad deals, conflict, or even scams. Here's how to negotiate confidently, safely, and fairly on MSukasha.

Good Bargaining Practices

  • Research the market price on MSukasha before making any offer.
  • Make your first offer 10–20% below asking price — not 50% lower.
  • Point out real, visible defects as justification for a lower price.
  • Be polite and respectful — you're building a transaction relationship.
  • Keep all negotiation inside MSukasha chat for dispute protection.
  • Agree on final price AND condition BEFORE meeting in person.

What to Avoid

  • Don't make insultingly low offers — sellers will block you.
  • Don't change the agreed price after meeting — this damages trust.
  • Don't negotiate after inspecting and agreeing unless a new defect is found.
  • Avoid sharing personal financial details during negotiation.
  • Never pay a "deposit" to hold an item to someone you haven't met.

The 3-Step Bargaining Script

1

Acknowledge the item positively

Example: "This looks in good condition. I'm genuinely interested."

2

Mention a specific concern as leverage

Example: "I noticed the battery is at 78% health — that means I'll need to replace it soon. Can we adjust the price to PKR X?"

3

Offer a clear final number and commit

Example: "My best offer is PKR 35,000 final. I have cash ready and can complete the deal today."

💡 Tip: The best deals go to buyers who are prepared, polite, and ready to close. Hesitation and back-and-forth over days often loses you the deal to someone else.
