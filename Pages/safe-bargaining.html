Safe Bargaining Tips

How to negotiate a fair price with local buyers and sellers on msukasha.

Negotiating in good faith

  • Research the typical price for similar items before making an offer.
  • Be polite and clear about your budget — most sellers are open to a fair offer.
  • Agree on the final price and payment method before meeting.

Red flags to watch for

A price that seems far below market value, pressure to pay before seeing the item, or a seller who avoids meeting in person are all reasons to be cautious.

If something feels off, it's always fine to walk away. There will be other listings.
