Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post a free ad on MSukasha?

Click "Post Free Ad" in the header or navigation. Fill in your item details, add photos, set your price, and publish. It is 100% free and takes less than 3 minutes.

What is Trade Assurance and how does it protect me?

Trade Assurance protects B2B buyers by holding payment in escrow until you confirm receipt of your goods as described. If there is a dispute, our team investigates and resolves it fairly.

How do I become a verified supplier?

Visit the Vendor Verification page, submit your business registration documents, NTN, and bank details. Our team reviews and approves verified suppliers within 3–5 business days.

How do I contact a seller or buyer?

Use the built-in messaging system by clicking "Message Seller" on any listing. You can also view the seller contact number if they have shared it publicly.
