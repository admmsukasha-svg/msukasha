Help Center

Answers to common questions about buying, selling and advertising on msukasha.

How do I post a free local ad?

Go to Post a Free Ad, fill in your item details and submit. It's free with no commission.

How does wholesale ordering work?

Browse the Wholesale (B2B) section, check the MOQ on a listing, then use "Inquire Now" or "Buy Now" to contact the supplier.

What is Trade Assurance?

A framework that helps protect wholesale orders on eligible listings. Read more on the Trade Assurance page.

How do I become a verified supplier?

Submit your business documents on the Start Verification page. Review usually takes 2–3 business days.

How do I advertise my business?

Visit Advertise With Us, choose a package and submit your ad for review.
