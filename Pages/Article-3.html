Understanding MSukasha Trade Assurance

May 3, 2026 3 min read MSukasha Team

One of the biggest concerns in B2B trade is payment security — what happens if you pay a supplier and the goods never arrive, or arrive in terrible condition? MSukasha Trade Assurance was built specifically to solve this problem. Here is everything you need to know about how it works and how it protects you.

What is Trade Assurance?

MSukasha Trade Assurance is a free payment protection program for B2B orders placed through the MSukasha platform. When you activate Trade Assurance on an order, your payment is held in a secure escrow account and only released to the supplier after you confirm that:

  • The goods were delivered on time (or within the agreed timeframe)
  • The products match the agreed specifications and quality
  • The quantity is correct as per the purchase order

If any of these conditions are not met, you are entitled to file a dispute and receive a full or partial refund.

How It Works — Step by Step

1. Place Order

Agree on specs, price, and timeline with supplier. Create a formal order on MSukasha.

2. Secure Payment

Your payment goes into escrow — held securely by MSukasha. Supplier is notified.

3. Production & Shipping

Supplier produces and ships goods. You can request inspection reports before dispatch.

4. Confirm & Release

You receive and verify goods. Confirm on MSukasha and payment is released to supplier.

What Does Trade Assurance Cover?

  • Late shipment: If goods arrive after the agreed delivery date, you can claim compensation.
  • Quality issues: If products don't match the sample or specifications agreed in writing, you're covered.
  • Short shipment: If you receive fewer units than ordered, the shortfall is refunded.
  • Non-delivery: If the supplier never ships, your payment is fully refunded.

💡 Important: Trade Assurance only applies to orders placed and paid through the MSukasha platform. Off-platform deals (e.g., direct bank transfers arranged outside MSukasha) are not covered.

How to Activate Trade Assurance on Your Order

Activating Trade Assurance is simple:

  • Go to the supplier's product page and click "Buy with Trade Assurance"
  • Fill in the order details: quantity, specs, delivery date
  • Complete payment through MSukasha's secure payment gateway
  • You will receive a Trade Assurance Order Number as confirmation

How to File a Dispute

If something goes wrong with your order, you have 30 days from the confirmed delivery date to file a dispute. Go to your order in My Account, click "Open Dispute," and upload evidence (photos, inspection reports, communication records). MSukasha's dispute resolution team reviews the case within 3–5 business days.

Trade Assurance vs. Standard Order — What's Different?

Standard orders on MSukasha have no payment protection — it's a direct transaction between you and the supplier. Trade Assurance adds MSukasha as a neutral third party holding the funds. For any order above PKR 50,000, we strongly recommend activating Trade Assurance. It is completely free for buyers.
