10 Safety Tips When Buying Used Electronics

May 2, 2026 4 min read MSukasha Team

Buying used electronics on MSukasha's C2C marketplace can save you thousands of rupees compared to buying new. But it also comes with risks — hidden damage, fake listings, or getting scammed. Follow these 10 tips to always buy smart and stay safe.

1

Always Inspect in Person Before Paying

Never pay for a used electronic item without seeing it physically first. Meet the seller in a safe public location — a busy café, a mall, or an MSukasha partner pickup point. Power on the device and test all features before handing over any money.

2

Check the IMEI or Serial Number

For phones and tablets, dial *#06# to get the IMEI number. Cross-check it with the box and original receipt if available. You can also check if the device is blacklisted (reported stolen) using Pakistan Telecom Authority's DIRBS portal.

3

Test Every Port, Button, and Function

Don't just check if it turns on. Test the charger port, headphone jack, front and back cameras, speakers, microphone, fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and all physical buttons. For laptops, check the keyboard, every USB port, and run a battery health check.

4

Check Battery Health

Battery degradation is the most common issue with used electronics. On iPhones, go to Settings → Battery → Battery Health. For Android phones, use apps like AccuBattery. Anything below 80% capacity means you'll need a battery replacement soon — factor that cost in.

⚠️ Warning: If a seller refuses to let you test the device before payment or insists on "online transfer first", that is a major red flag. Walk away immediately.

5

Look for Physical Damage Under Good Light

Take the device into direct sunlight or bright light to spot hairline cracks on the screen, scratches on the lens, dents on edges, or signs of water damage (look for rust inside ports or a red water indicator inside the SIM tray).

6

Factory Reset and Re-Setup During Inspection

Ask the seller to factory reset the device in front of you and set it up fresh. This confirms the device is not iCloud locked (for iPhones), not Google account locked (for Androids), and has no hidden spyware or tracking software installed.

7

Buy from Verified Sellers When Possible

MSukasha's C2C marketplace shows seller ratings, review history, and verified badge status. Prioritize sellers with 4+ stars and at least 10 completed transactions. A verified seller has gone through MSukasha's identity check process.

8

Never Pay Full Amount Before Delivery

For deliveries, use MSukasha's escrow payment option — your money is held securely and only released to the seller once you confirm receipt and satisfaction. Never do a direct bank transfer to an unknown seller.

9

Research the Market Price First

Before meeting any seller, check similar listings on MSukasha to know the going rate for that model and condition. If someone is selling a phone at a price 40–50% below market, it is either stolen, broken, or a scam.

10

Report Suspicious Listings Immediately

If you encounter a fake listing, a scammer, or a stolen device, report it immediately using the flag button on the listing page or contact MSukasha Support. Keeping the marketplace safe is everyone's responsibility.
