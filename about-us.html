Our Story

Empowering Trade

MSukasha was founded with a simple mission: to make trade easier, safer, and more accessible for every Pakistani — from factory owners sourcing in bulk to individuals selling a used laptop from their home.

We combine the power of B2B wholesale sourcing with the simplicity of C2C local classifieds, all under one trusted platform backed by trade assurance and verified supplier programs.
