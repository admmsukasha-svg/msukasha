Your Money is Safe with Us

MSukasha Trade Assurance holds your payment in secure escrow until you confirm the goods have been received exactly as described.

Step 1 — Secure Payment

Place your order. Your payment is held in MSukasha's secure escrow account — the supplier never receives it until you confirm delivery.

Step 2 — Shipment & Tracking

The supplier ships your order. You can track shipment status in real time from your account dashboard.

Step 3 — Confirm & Release

You inspect the goods. If everything matches — confirm receipt and the payment is released to the supplier. If there's an issue, open a dispute.
