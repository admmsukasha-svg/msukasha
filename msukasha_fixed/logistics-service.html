Our Services

Express Parcel Delivery

Same-day and next-day delivery for parcels up to 30kg across all major cities. Tracked end-to-end via MSukasha dashboard.

Bulk Freight (FTL/LTL)

Full and partial truckload services for wholesale orders. Dedicated trucks or shared loads depending on your volume. Door-to-door service available.

Warehousing & Fulfillment

Store your inventory in MSukasha's partner warehouses in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. We pick, pack, and dispatch orders on your behalf.

Air Cargo (Export/Import)

Fast international air freight from Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad airports. Customs clearance and door delivery included.

Sea Freight (FCL/LCL)

Cost-effective ocean freight from Karachi Port for large volume exports. Full container or shared container loads handled from booking to delivery.

Returns Management

Seamless reverse logistics for B2B and C2C orders. Scheduled pickups, condition assessment at warehouse, and fast restocking or refunds.
